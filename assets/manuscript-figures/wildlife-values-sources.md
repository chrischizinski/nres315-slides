# Wildlife value orientation maps

These external research maps were added at the instructor's request on
2026-10-05 to supplement the Chapter 8 psychology lecture. Original figure
content is unchanged.

## United States: Mutualist type, 2018

- Asset: `awv-mutualists-us-2018.jpg`.
- Source: CSU-led *America's Wildlife Values* national report, Map 2,
  printed page 15 (PDF page 15).
- Report: <https://sites.warnercnr.colostate.edu/wildlifevalues/wp-content/uploads/sites/124/2019/01/AWV-National-Final-Report.pdf>.
- Project and methods: <https://sites.warnercnr.colostate.edu/wildlifevalues/results/>.
- Original JPEG extracted from the PDF with `pdfimages -f 15 -l 15 -j`;
  the second embedded image is Map 2. No cropping or recoloring.
- Survey: 2018; 43,949 respondents in all 50 states.
- Metric: percentage classified as Mutualists (high mutualism, low
  domination), excluding Pluralists.
- Slide text rounds Nebraska 29.1%, California 47.6%, and Wyoming 21.9%
  to whole percentages. Original map retains its labels and legend.

## International: two orientations, 2021-2023

- Asset: `manfredo-teel-wildlife-values-33-countries-2026.png`.
- Source: Manfredo, M. J., Teel, T. L., Ghasemi, B., et al. (2026).
  *Enduring cultural legacies affect Euro-American wildlife values*.
  *Nature Sustainability*, 9, 970-980. Figure 1.
- Article: <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41893-026-01825-8>.
- Figure and caption: <https://www.nature.com/articles/s41893-026-01825-8/figures/1>.
- Original image: <https://media.springernature.com/full/springer-static/image/art%3A10.1038%2Fs41893-026-01825-8/MediaObjects/41893_2026_1825_Fig1_HTML.png>.
- Survey: 2021-2023; 18,477 respondents across 33 countries in Europe
  and the Americas. This is not worldwide coverage.
- Metric: percentages above 4.5 on each orientation scale, measured
  separately; residents can be counted in both panels. These percentages
  are not the same four-type metric as the U.S. report's Mutualist category.
- Caption states that overseas territories/departments use values from their
  sovereign states. Unsurveyed countries are gray.
- Original publisher figure downloaded unchanged. Credit: Manfredo, Teel,
  and colleagues / Nature Sustainability (Springer Nature). Rights remain
  with the original rights holders.
